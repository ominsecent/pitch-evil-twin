    About Evil Twin Capital

    Evil Twin Capital is an investor-operator platform founded by twin brothers Jason and Matthew Goldman. It’s built on their multi-generational San Francisco roots and a deep network of relationships, with a seed to late stage fund-of-funds at its core. ETC invests across venture funds, emerging managers, solo GPs, spin-out funds and hacker houses. It also invests directly on company cap tables and buys secondaries, which gives it exposure to hundreds of startups. The focus is AI infrastructure and autonomous agents, vertical AI, defense and dual-use technology, cybersecurity, fintech, aviation, robotics, space, frontier tech and other breakout private companies. A core goal is to find and share the companies and opportunities its large GP network is quietly converging on. Jason is a founder and operator who, with Matthew, co-founded G2 and sold it to private equity. The brothers are also community leaders who run the Stern Grove Festival.

    Pitches are screened with AI assistance and read by Jason and the team.
